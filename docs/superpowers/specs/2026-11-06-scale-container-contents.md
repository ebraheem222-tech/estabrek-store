# Scale container contents

The owner explicitly wants growing/shrinking a container to grow/shrink its descendants with it, rather than a child resizing its parent. Apply the same proportion to the whole group, including nested containers, text, images and widgets. Children remain independently editable. Keep a visible option to resize only the container frame.

Use the existing native positive uniform `scale` transform and measured raw dimensions, rather than rewriting every descendant's responsive styles. A uniform transform preserves fonts, padding, widgets, nested transforms and locked-child styling faithfully; independently rewriting lengths would miss intrinsic and dynamic content. Group resize keeps proportions even on side handles. Freeze each gesture's original geometry and clamp its ratio to the existing 0.1–4 authored-scale range. Preserve the opposite edge, authored origin/rotation, zoom, RTL, cancellation and one-step undo/redo. Preview the real group live.

The browser preference **Scale contents** defaults on for nonempty native containers, and can be switched off to retain existing frame resizing. Preserve the container's original positioning and raw width/height semantics during group scaling: flow stays flow, free stays free, auto stays auto and percentage dimensions keep their containing block. This prevents relayout of intrinsic and percentage-height descendants. No schema changes, dependencies, storefront changes or merchant data writes are required. A natural-size reset releases group scale when this option is active. Continue the already authorized main/live deployment workflow after tests and review.
